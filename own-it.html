Common questions

What kinds of software can become hardware?

Tools that perform one simple, repetitive job locally — monitoring, dashboards, alerts, counters, attendance, inventory, logging, automation and small business tools. We assess each submission honestly; not every SaaS can or should become a box.

What does it cost to explore an idea?

Nothing. Tell us the job and we'll give you an honest feasibility assessment. Only if we build the hardware is there a cost, quoted upfront before anything starts.

How long does a custom build take?

Depends on the job. Simple devices can go from concept to prototype in a few weeks. Firmware-heavy products take longer. We'll give you a timeline with the quote.

Do the devices need the internet?

No. CktHub devices are designed for local operation — data stays on the device or your network. Internet is optional, and only where a specific feature genuinely needs it.

What if hardware doesn't make sense for my case?

We'll tell you. Sometimes software genuinely is the right answer — we'd rather lose a project than sell you something that doesn't fit. That honesty is why the business works.
