02 — The philosophy

What if software didn't need a subscription?

Lots of tools perform simple, repetitive jobs: watch a sensor, count something, show a number, log an event. Those jobs can run on a box sitting next to you instead of a server billing you monthly.

monitoringdashboardsalertscounters attendanceinventoryloggingautomation local data collectioncontrol systemssmall business tools

Not every SaaS product can or should become hardware — big analytics, collaboration and anything that genuinely needs a network of servers stays in software. But for the jobs that are really just a sensor, a screen, and a bit of logic, the subscription is the expensive part.

Use software when software makes sense. Own hardware when the job doesn't need a subscription. That's the whole idea — nothing ideological about it.
