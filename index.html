Why CktHub

Real questions, answered plainly.

Why are you doing this?

Because most simple tools are already subscription software. We build the hardware that does the same job without the recurring bill.

Do you make devices that replace SaaS?

Yes. We design purpose-built boxes that take over the work of subscription tools, and the firmware runs on the device itself, no cloud required.

How can I buy a device?

Store orders for the boards on this page. For a custom device, tell us the job, we will quote you a one-time price.

Who makes the products?

We do. PCB design, SMT assembly and firmware are done in-house by Ambivert Technologies Pvt Ltd.

Who is this for?

People who run a business and people who build things. Shopkeepers, makers, engineers, tinkerers, facility managers.

Do you offer support?

Yes, on WhatsApp. We help you wire it, flash it, and get the job running.
